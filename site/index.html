What is Peptendium?＋

Peptendium is an educational peptide reference and research-literacy app. It combines peptide profiles, evidence context, research highlights, Academy lessons, and a personal notebook.

Can I explore it for free?＋

Yes. Free access includes core peptide reference content and educational tools. Peptendium Pro offers a one-time lifetime unlock for additional research and study features. See the app for current pricing.

Who is it for?＋

Students, researchers, and curious learners who want a clearer way to explore peptide science and understand research evidence.

Does it offer dosing or treatment guidance?＋

No. Peptendium is for education. It does not provide dosing instructions, protocols, prescribing, or treatment recommendations. Educational content should be independently verified.

Where is my notebook stored?＋

Your research workspace is stored on your device. It brings saved profiles, collections, and personal notes together in the app.

Where can I get support?＋

Visit Ocoee Studios for product support and feedback. Mention Peptendium so we can help with the right app.
